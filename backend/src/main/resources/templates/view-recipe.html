Cozy Pumpkin Soup

A silky, warmly spiced soup for crisp autumn evenings.

Easy Dinner Autumn Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pumpkin puree
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • to taste cream

Notes

  • Top with toasted pumpkin seeds and a swirl of cream.

Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. 2. Add the onion and garlic; cook until soft and fragrant. 3. Stir in the pumpkin, broth, and spices. Simmer for 20 minutes. 4. Blend until smooth, then stir in the cream and season to taste.
